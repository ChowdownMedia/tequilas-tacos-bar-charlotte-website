The showstopper

Molcajete Perrón

A true showstopper of Mexican cuisine, served sizzling in a traditional stone mortar that locks in its bold, smoky flavors. Tender steak, juicy chicken and succulent shrimp with grilled cactus, onions and peppers, topped with bubbling melted cheese — finished with fresh guacamole, salsa and warm tortillas.

Seafood Delight, Louisiana-Style

A vibrant seafood dish that celebrates the coastal flavors of Mexico. Fresh seafood seasoned with traditional Mexican spices, cooked to tender perfection and complemented by rich, smoky notes — garnished with bright, zesty toppings and a side of Mexican-style slaw. A refreshing taste of the ocean in every savory bite.
